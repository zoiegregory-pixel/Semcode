# 📜 Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose
Our project team is committed to fostering an open, welcoming, and respectful environment. This Code of Conduct outlines expected behaviors and responsibilities to ensure effective collaboration.

## 2. Expected Behavior
- **Respectful Communication**: Use professional, constructive language in all discussions.
- **Collaboration**: Support teammates, share knowledge, and work towards common goals.
- **Inclusivity**: Value diverse perspectives and ensure everyone feels included.
- **Accountability**: Take responsibility for your contributions and meet agreed deadlines.
- **Transparency**: Document decisions clearly in GitHub Issues, commits, and pull requests.

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior
- Discrimination, harassment, or offensive comments.
- Dismissing or ignoring contributions from team members.
- Plagiarism or misrepresentation of work.
- Deliberate disruption of workflows or CI/CD pipelines.
- Ignoring agreed sprint tasks or backlog responsibilities.

## 4. Responsibilities
- **Project Owner**: Ensures backlog clarity and prioritization.
- **Scrum Master**: Facilitates meetings, monitors adherence to this Code, and resolves conflicts.
- **Team Members**: Follow agile practices, respect GitFlow, and contribute to sprint goals.

## 5. Enforcement
- Issues should be reported to the Scrum Master.
- Minor infractions → documented in sprint retrospective.
- Major infractions → escalated to course instructor/assessment supervisor.

## 6. Attribution
This Code of Conduct is adapted from Unit 10a guidelines and tailored for our DevOps assessment project.
